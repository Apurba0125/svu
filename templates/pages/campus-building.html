HOW THE VIEWER IS WIRED data-viewer on the grid makes it one gallery; data-viewer-item on each cell makes it a frame. The viewer (counter, fullscreen, close, arrows, caption, keyboard, swipe) is built by main.js and styled in base.css, shared by every gallery page. The caption under an enlarged photograph is that image's own alt text, so writing a good alt does double duty. !!
