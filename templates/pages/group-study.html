HOW THE VIEWER IS WIRED data-viewer on the grid makes it one gallery; data-viewer-item on each cell makes it a frame. That is the whole contract — no onclick attributes and no index numbers to keep in step, so inserting a photograph in the middle does not renumber anything after it. The viewer (counter, fullscreen, close, arrows, caption, keyboard, swipe) is built by main.js and styled in base.css, shared by every gallery page. The caption under an enlarged photograph is that image's own alt text, so writing a good alt does double duty. With one photograph the viewer hides its arrows and counter on its own; they come back as soon as there are two. !! CAPTIONS STILL TO WRITE The three AVIF files could not be opened to describe them.
